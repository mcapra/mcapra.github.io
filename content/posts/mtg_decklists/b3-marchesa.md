## Overview
The purpose of this deck is to make sure that every other deck on the table is doing what their deck is aiming to do and to play their biggest threats, build a massive boardstate, cast a big finishing spell, etc .. that we can use it against them (Aikido). Thats why we are trying to give some resources to the others for example by making them draw cards and if someone's too far ahead we'll try to slow them down a bit by removing some big boys or important combo pieces. **When building your deck, be sure to never give your opponent draw AND ramp!**
### You should play this deck if you 👍:
1. **take great pleasure in trying to ensure that everyone is having a great time by making them achieve what their decks are aiming to do before the game ends.**
2. love reactive/interactive decks.
3. like having an answer for nearly every situation.
4. enjoy letting you opponents think that they are going to win the game while planning their downfall by using their strength against them.
5. love a mixture between playing aggressively/defensively and trying to judge when each is the best play.
6. have the patience to wait for an opportunity to take control over the game.
### You souldn't play this deck if you 👎:
1. need to declare a lot of big creatures as attackers every single turn.
2. only like straight forward/linear decks.
3. don't like Decks with a learning-curve.
4. need a reliable combo-wincon.
5. are impatient.

## Gameplan
![Gameplan](https://i.gifer.com/7BBW.gif)

With this deck you are always going to be on the edge of winning or losing and that's what makes this deck so exciting for me.

**Conclusion:**
* Analyse the biggest threat, set up some kind of pillowfort and draw *(Early-Game)*
* Encourage the others to fight against each other/hurt each other and eliminate the most threatening player (at least the most threatening for us :) ) *(Mid-Game)*
* Take control over the game and win it with our opponents momentum *(Late-Game)*
### Early-Game
Starting with the early-game we should talk about our starting hand. You should definitely have access to all of our colors in your starting hand weather it contains simple lands or artifact-ramp. Having some sort of card draw and pillowfort effects on top would be very nice.

While the others reveal their commander you should take a look at their archetypes and determine who should be the number one target to focus first and who is going to help you to accomplish that. We want to set up some kind of pillowfort cards like Ghostly Prison, Windborn Muse or even a flying/deathtouch-body is going to help us a lot like Nighthawk Scavenger or Vampire Nighthawk to prevent people from attacking us. Having access to card draw would be great as well and the optimum would be political card draw like Flumph, Cut a Deal or Humble Defector. Breena, the Demagogue would combine the pillowfort and draw aspect. Since our beloved Queen Marchesa is a 3/3 deathtouch-body and granting us the Monarch she would be the backup to give us a deathtouch-blocker or some draw.

### Mid-Game
Entering the mid-game means that we should find a way to encourage the others to attack/hurt each other and do the dirty work for us. We could play Kardur, Doomscourge or try to hurt the others while they are fighting each other by using Duelist's Heritage. Gisela, Blade of Goldnight would encourage them to fight each other too. Adding the Monarch to the game by casting Queen Marchesa and giving it away would make the others fight each other aswell. At the transition between the mid- and late-game we should have eliminated the most dangerous player in the game for us. We could try to make our most threatening enemy even more threatening for the others by letting him tutor a card with Scheming Symmetry or let him draw cards with Secret Rendezvous to get some help from them.

### Late-Game
Now its time for us to close out the game and finish the others with their own threats. The best case scenario would be to get our Sunforger-Toolbox online by just casting him or even tutoring for it with cards like Scheming Symmetry or Wishclaw Talisman (Fighter Class, Open the Armory, ...). Once the Sunforger is attached our enemies are going to have a hard time to remove it, since we could respond to a removal by unattaching and protecting it. Is your last opponent someone who has a lot of creatures on the field - kill him with Rakdos Charm, Mob Rule or Inkshield. Does your last enemy have some big enchanted creatures or just some big "timmy"-boys on the field - Finish him with Backlash/Delirium or even Brash Taunter. Is the last opponent standing someone who tries to finish you with one single X-Spell or even an Aetherflux Reservoir - Return it to the sender with Deflecting Palm/Comeuppance or even Selfless Squire+Rogue's Passage.

## Sunforger-Toolbox

![Sunforger](https://i.ibb.co/4PjZrbG/thor-sunforger-300.gif) 

In my opinion the Sunforger is one of the best boros cards and an auto-include for every interactive/reactive Deck. This {R}{W}-equipment gives us the ability to have an answer to almost any threat at the table. The following list is a breakdown what every single card in the sunforger toolbox should be doing.
___
Angel's Grace - Just a way to survive a lethal attack or to prevent the opponent from winning on the spot, for example with Approach of the second sun, Thassa's Oracle, ...

Anguished Unmaking - Incredible spot removal that gets around indestructible and graveyard-strategys

Arcbond - Can be used to kill enemies while they are fighting each other but the main goal of this card is to use it when our deathtouch/lifelink creatures (Nighthawk Scavenger, Vampire Nighthawk, Verrak, Warped Sengir) are taking damage which will cause a boardwipe, massive lifegain and maybe even lethal damage. Can even be used on a big Selfless Squire and in Combination with Blasphemous Act aswell. - **Keep in mind that we are going to take the damage too!**

Backlash, Delirium - Is your opponent trying to kill you with a 21 power Commander or a huge Managorger Hydra? Well, not today!

Boros Charm - Very versatile card: Save your cards from a boardwipe, remove a planeswalker, kill an opponent with four or less life (with Gisela, Blade of Goldnight eight or less life) or give an attacker double strike to finish someone off, doesnt need to be yours

Chaos Warp - Versatile spot removal that gets around indestructible

Comeuppance - Protect yourself from a deadly attack and kill the attacking creatures or reflect big  damage spells like Crackle with Power, Comet Storm, Aetherflux Reservoir,...

Crackling Doom - Great spell to handle multiple threats at once

Deflecting Palm - In my opinion, this card is the reincarnation of classical Aikido - protect yourself from cards like Crackle with Power, Comet Storm, Aetherflux Reservoir,... and fire it back

Display of Power - Just as flexible as Reverberate, can copy every instant or sorcery spell you'd like to BUT this spell is able to **copy** the **whole stack** and not just a single spell!

Generous Gift - Versatile spot removal, gives an opponent a 3/3 attacker/blocker but is able to hit dangerous lands like Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx, Cabal Coffers, etc .. aswell.

Hide // Seek - Is your opponent trying to search his library for his last combo piece or has cast Approach of the second sun for the first time? Well, we are going to find the card earlier and remove it from the game. Can be used to remove an artifact or enchantment aswell and even to handle problematic indestructible artifacts/enchantments like Purphoros, God of the forge, Heliod, Sun-Crowned, ..

Lapse of Certainty, Tibalt's Trickery, Mana Tithe - Classic counterspells which will surprise some opponents because they dont expect a counterspell from a mardu deck

Olórin's Searing Light - A removal spell that exiles and is able to bypass ward, hexproof (+indestructible) + hits each opponents "strongest" creature + will potentially kill someone or maybe even win us the game by killing every opponent in an aikido-ish way -> Do I need to say anything else? 

Path to Exile, Swords to Plowshares - Best creature removal that gets around indestructible

Rakdos Charm - Classic Graveyard-hate and artifact removal. Can be used to kill go-wide strategy-based decks

Reverberate - Very flexible spell, copy whatever you want: Maybe a counterspell that is trying to aim at one of your spells or kill someone with his own Torment of Hailfire, Exsanguinate, ... 

Stroke of Midnight - Versatile spot removal and gives an opponent a 1/1 attacker/blocker but isn't able to hit lands.

Valakut Awakening - Flexible card that takes a land slot and can be used to draw a new hand of cards (+1) if needed

Wear // Tear - Simple artifact and enchantment removal
___
Mistveil Plains - This land opens up the possibility for us to put one card from our graveyard back into the library (normally once per round). This leads to the fact that we can use the cards from our (Sunforger-) Toolbox in connection with the Sunforger over and over again.

## Power-Level
It is not very easy and kind of controversial to rank a deck in a power level but in the following I'm going to try to talk about the power-level of the deck and for a better understanding im reffering to this [Graphic](https://imgur.com/OcMdyUH).

![Vegeta, Its over 9000](https://media.tenor.com/TKKlYOpCbV8AAAAC/dragon-ball-z-dbz.gif)

Since we are trying to beat our opponents with their own momentum, this deck should be able to scale with the decks of our opponents but only up to a certain point. Competitive decks (power level 8 and above) are probably going to be too fast for us however, this also means that this deck can easily be played against weaker decks without generating much frustration by being too strong, as for example our Inkshield won't have a big impact on the game as long as the opponent can't deal a lot of damage to us, same is true for Delirium/Backlash, Deflecting Palm, etc.. 

## Weaknesses
![Madara, Weakness](https://media.tenor.com/sFct6jVywUgAAAAC/weakness-disgusts-me-senju.gif)

This deck is very flexible and able to play against a lot of different deck-archetypes and powerlevels but of course isn't able to cope with everything.

**The biggest enemies of this deck are the following archetypes:**
* Aristocrats (Lifeloss Strategys in general)
* Mill
* (Fast) Infinite Combos
