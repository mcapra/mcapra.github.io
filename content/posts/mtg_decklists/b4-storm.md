## The Commander
 
**Storm, Force of Nature** is a 4-mana 3/4 Mutant Hero with flying and vigilance. Her ability, *Ceaseless Tempest*, reads: whenever Storm deals combat damage to a player, the next instant or sorcery spell you cast that turn gains **storm** — meaning it is copied once for every spell cast earlier in the turn.
 
In other words, she turns any spell in the deck into a potential game-ending haymaker, provided you can connect in combat and build a high spell count first.
 
## The Game Plan
 
The deck operates in three phases:
 
### 1. Ramp fast and deploy Storm early
 
A suite of cheap mana creatures — Arbor Elf, Birds of Paradise, Bloom Tender, and Delighted Halfling — plus green ramp sorceries like Farseek accelerate Storm onto the battlefield ahead of schedule. Anger (discarded to the graveyard) grants her haste so she can attack the same turn she arrives.
 
### 2. Connect in combat and chain cheap spells
 
Once Storm deals combat damage to a player, the engine turns on. The deck fires off a flurry of cantrips and rituals — Gitaxian Probe, Expressive Iteration, Jeska's Will, Mana Geyser, and similar — to inflate the storm count while netting mana and cards along the way. Payoff creatures keep the chain alive:
 
- **Birgi, God of Storytelling** refunds a red mana per spell cast
- **Storm-Kiln Artist** creates a Treasure token per instant or sorcery
- **Archmage Emeritus** draws a card for each instant or sorcery
- **Snapcaster Mage** rebuys key spells from the graveyard for extra value

### 3. Cash in with one massively copied spell
 
The finisher is a single powerful spell cast with the commander-granted storm trigger, copied a dozen or more times. Mizzix's Mastery — which recasts your entire graveyard of instants and sorceries for free — is the signature kill shot; mass-damage effects (e.g., Blasphemous Act, End the Festivities) and extra-turn spells serve similar roles. Copying an extra-turn spell several times typically chains into further extra turns until opponents are defeated or concede.
 
## Strengths
 
- Explosive, often single-turn wins once the engine is assembled
- The commander converts *any* spell into a threat, giving the deck flexible win angles
- Ramp and cantrip density make the game plan consistent

## Weaknesses
 
- **Combat-dependent:** everything hinges on Storm surviving and connecting with an opponent; repeated commander removal or a well-timed fog blanks the whole turn
- **Disruption-prone:** the deck relies on resolving many spells in one turn, so counterspells and stack interaction during the storm turn can be devastating
- **Table threat:** as a known storm deck, it draws heavy attention and removal from the whole pod

## TL;DR
 
Ramp → attack with Storm → chain cheap spells to build the storm count → copy one devastating spell many times to win, usually in a single explosive turn.
