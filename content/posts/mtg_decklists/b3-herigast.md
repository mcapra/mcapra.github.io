# Primer

Ever since it's spoiler release, I couldn't keep my eyes off Herigast, Erupting Nullkite. The art, the typing, the abilities, the fact it was monored. Everything was just right. Fast forward a month after it's release and I already had my paper deck ready to play. 

Nothing like a mono red aristocrat deck to get the ol' neurons connecting. The deck plays a lot like a creature storm + burn deck so if you like taking 15 minute turns out of nowhere this is your deck 😂 get ready to get some eye rolls when you say "I think I won" with just your commander and a value piece on the board 👀😂

Also, with all these burning effects I couldn't think of a more fitting name for the deck than Avenged Sevenfold's "Shepherd of Fire 🔥" 🤘🏻

## ⭐ Main gameplan line

Before explaining the deck, what is ***Emerge***? It's an ability that lets you sacrifice a creature to reduce the casting cost of a creature by it's own mana value. So for example, sacrificing a Rekindling Phoenix reduces the creature you're casting by {4} mana. The subtilty lies on the fact that you can't reduced colored mana. This, is going to be important at some point. Also, Herigast does not care where you cast the creature from, it still gives them emerge.

The main gameplan of the deck can be divided into 4 steps:

- The setup turns: play your early ramp creatures like Ornithopter of Paradise, Iron Myr or Zookeeper Mechan. Then play your beefier ramp creatures or creatures that you get value put of them dying when you cast Herigast like Solemn Simulacrum, Atsushi, the Blazing Sky.
- Then play your value pieces like Glaring Fleshraker, Skittering Precursor or of course Skullclamp.
- Then Herigast emerges and you just try to chain your creature casts and try to draw either one of your burn effects with cards like Ruin Grinder which you basically cast for {R}.
- Finally, you play your burn effects like Terror of the Peaks, Screamer-Killer or Sarkhan's Unsealing. And then just keep on looping your creatures with the help of cards like Myr Retriever. I do have to mention the fact that you can "accidentally" combo with the Retriever, Scrap Trawler, Glaring Fleshraker and another artifact creature. Add a pinger and it's insta win.

## 💡Alternative lines and high availability

The plan B of the deck basically revolves around Feldon of the Third Path and Jaxis, the Troublemaker. Making copies of chunky creatures and attacking, but to be honest I have yet to win once via combat damage.

Industrial Advancement and Sunbird's Invocation are also two ways to generate value in case Herigast gets removed often but from experience with the deck you can easily recast him after being removed 3 times thanks to it's Emerge cost. Also cards like Mimic Vat can help you get a {9} drop on board for {3} mana.

## 🚧 Single points of failure

The best card IMO in the deck and the only one that cannot be replaced, the one, the only, the card that should be a gamechanger: Skullclamp. This card is just a cheat code in this deck. Have one of the eldrazi scion generators on board while sacrificing your creatures and it's basically a draw 2 for 0 mana. The deck being mono red struggles sometimes with cards advantage, so losing skullclamp is basically going half a bracket down in power sometimes. Makes the deck go from "I win this turn" to "I don't know if I'll be able to kill more than one opponent". ⚠️ So play the card with a lot of precaution in removal heavy pods!!

## 💭 Deckbuilding worries

The deck lacks some interactions and card draw. For interactions if say more specifically protection spells rather than removal. Bolt bend and Redirect Lightning could be good inclusions probably. With a higher budget Tibalt's Trickery is a no brainer. For the card draw you'll be limited since you'll want them as much as possible in either creature form, or cards that give you insane card advantage like Zenith Festival cast using harmonize or Imposing Grandeur.

The deck can also have some issues with consistency as like mentioned previously, Skullclamp changes your game entirely. And so do certain cards like the Fleshraker. Additionally, Terror of the Peaks is both the best and cheapest option out of the burn effects, so the others may feel a bit awkward to play around at times.

Finally, one ressource you'll often run of is...{R}. Yup, creatures needing the red pip will be stuck in your hand at some point, which is why it's important to run cards like Patron of the Arts and Atsushi.

## 🪶 Final note

What can I say about this deck without under selling it? It's one of my favorite decks to play. People usually do not grasp how strong emerge can be until it's too late. Adding cards to play the emerged cards at instant speed makes you feel like you're cheating basically. 

It's a dragon 🐉. It's an eldrazi 🐙. It's red {R}. It's colorless {C}. It's basically Henzie "Toolbox" Torre in mono red mixed with aristocrat gameplay. I think it's the kind of deck you either like, or are 0% interested in playing. As for me, it's my first, best and most favorite mono red deck.
